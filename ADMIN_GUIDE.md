# 🛡️ Rhockstar Connect — SuperAdmin Operating Manual

This guide outlines daily operational workflows for platform managers and SuperAdmins on **Rhockstar Connect**.

---

## 📌 1. Advertisement Moderation & Review (`/admin/ads`)

### Reviewing Submissions
1. Log in with an Admin account and navigate to **SuperAdmin Dashboard -> Advertisements** (`/admin/ads`).
2. Filter by **Pending (Review)** tab.
3. Inspect ad title, content, target link, and media preview.

### Approving an Advert
- Click **`[✓ Approve Advert]`**.
- Set campaign pricing (Default: ₦15,000 / week).
- **Result**: Ad status changes to `approved`. The advertiser receives an automated email notification to pay and activate.

### Rejecting an Advert
- Click **`[✕ Reject]`**.
- Enter a clear rejection reason (e.g., *"Low resolution image"*, *"Inappropriate language"*).
- **Result**: Ad status changes to `rejected`. The advertiser is notified by email. **No payment is taken.**

---

## 📌 2. Managing Active Live Campaigns

- **Pausing a Campaign**: If an ad needs temporary suspension, click **`[Pause]`**. The ad immediately stops appearing in the Feed stream.
- **Resuming a Campaign**: Click **`[Resume Live]`** to reactivate.
- **Performance Metrics**: View real-time impressions (views) and click-through counts per campaign directly on the admin table.

---

## 📌 3. Content Reports & User Moderation (`/admin/reports`)

1. Review reported posts or flagged users in `/admin/reports`.
2. To remove a post that violates community guidelines, click **`[Delete Post]`**.
3. To suspend a disruptive user, update their account status in `/admin/users`.
